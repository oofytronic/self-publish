1
Before you touch KDP — gather everything
Collect the pieces first so you never have to stop halfway through a form.
1
Make a “Book 2” folder with these files

Put everything in one folder on your computer. Tick each one as you finish it — your ticks are saved on this device.

ⓘSelling both formats is a good idea: KDP lets you link them on one Amazon page so readers can pick either. They’re set up as two “formats” of the same book.
2
Decide the key numbers: trim size, paper, page count

Match what you did for Book 1 so the two books look like a set. Open Book 1 in your KDP Bookshelf → Edit paperback details/content and copy down its trim size and paper color. Your final page count is shown after you finish formatting — you need it for the cover spine.

✦Not sure? Copy Book 1 exactly. Looking at your first book’s page on KDP costs nothing and changes nothing as long as you don’t click Publish.
2
Format the manuscript
Paperback and Kindle are formatted differently, so this happens twice.
1
Paperback interior

KDP takes a Word .docx or a PDF. Set the page size to your trim size (Layout → Size → More paper sizes). Margins: at least 0.5" top/bottom/outside and 0.75" on the inside (spine) edge — more inside margin for books over ~300 pages. Use Heading 1 for chapter titles, and make the first page of each chapter start on a new page.

Easiest route: in KDP go to Help → Formatting a paperback manuscript, download the free Word template for your trim size, and paste your text into it.

✦Ask the person who helped with Book 1 for the final interior file. Reusing its styles is the quickest way to make Book 2 match.
2
Kindle eBook interior

Upload a clean .docx using Heading 1 for chapter titles and a real (automatic) table of contents. No manual page numbers or fixed layouts — Kindle text reflows. For a preview of how it will look on a device, use the free Kindle Create app, or the online previewer inside KDP.

3
Make the cover files in Canva ⚠ sizes must be exact
Three different image files. Canva sizes are given below — the calculator works out the exact numbers for your book.
1
Cover file 1 — Kindle eBook cover (front only)

The simplest one. In Canva: Create a design → Custom size, enter 1600 × 2560 px. Design the front cover, then Share → Download → JPG (highest quality).

ⓘKDP’s minimum is 1,000 px on the longest side and its recommended size is 2,560 × 1,600 px. Using 1600 × 2560 satisfies both.
2
Cover file 2 — Paperback full wrap cover (front + spine + back)

This is a single wide image for the whole outside of the book. It must be sized to your exact trim size and page count or KDP will reject it. Use the calculator below to get the Canva custom size in inches, then:

  1. In Canva choose Create a design → Custom size, switch units to in, and type the width and height from the calculator.
  2. Turn on View → Show rulers and guides. Drag guides to mark the spine (left and right edges) and the 0.125" bleed on every outer edge.
  3. Back cover goes on the left, spine in the middle, front cover on the right. Keep all text and logos at least 0.25" away from every trim edge and the spine fold.
  4. Leave a clear 2" × 1.2" box in the lower-right of the back cover — Amazon prints the barcode there. Put nothing important in it.
  5. Share → Download → PDF Print, quality set to highest. Leave Crop marks and bleed unticked — the bleed is already built into your size.
!Spine text is only possible at about 79+ pages. Below that, leave the spine blank. Also: if the front art was made at 72 DPI or is small, enlarging it makes it blurry — check the original’s pixel size first.

4
Create Book 2 on KDP — step by step
Take it one page at a time. Nothing is public until the very last click.
1
Start a new title (this does not touch Book 1)

Go to kdp.amazon.com → Bookshelf → + Create, then choose Paperback (or Kindle eBook). Book 1 stays exactly as it is — “Create” always makes a brand-new title.

✦Nothing you do here goes live by accident. A new book stays a draft until you click the final “Publish” button on the Pricing page. You can save, close the browser and come back any time.
!Be careful to use + Create, not “Edit” beside Book 1. Editing Book 1 and clicking Publish changes the live book.
2
Page 1: Paperback Details

Fill in each field:

  • Language — English.
  • Book title / subtitle — must exactly match the title on the cover and title page.
  • Series — only if this is part of a series. If so, link it to Book 1 here.
  • Author — same pen name as Book 1 so both land on one Author page.
  • Description — paste your blurb (you can use simple bold/italics).
  • Publishing rights — “I own the copyright”.
  • Keywords — your 7 phrases (see Phase 5).
  • Categories — pick up to 3 (see Phase 5).
  • Age range / adult content — answer honestly.

Click Save and Continue at the bottom. Just closing the page does not save everything.

3
Page 2: Paperback Content

In order:

  1. ISBN — click “Assign me a free KDP ISBN” (easiest; Amazon is then listed as publisher), or use your own if you bought one.
  2. Print options — Interior: black & white; paper: white or cream (as decided); trim size as decided; bleed: “No bleed” for text-only interiors; cover finish: matte or glossy (match Book 1).
  3. Manuscript — upload your .docx or PDF.
  4. Book cover — choose “Upload a cover you already have (print-ready PDF only)” and upload your Canva PDF.
  5. AI-generated content — answer the question honestly (Amazon requires disclosure of AI-generated text/images/translations).
  6. Click Launch Previewer.
4
Use the Previewer — your safety check

KDP shows errors (red) and warnings (yellow). Page through every page, especially the first pages, chapter starts, and the cover.

  • “Cover is the wrong size” — your page count or trim size doesn’t match what the calculator used. Re-run it with the page count KDP shows and resize in Canva.
  • Text outside margins — adjust margins in Word and re-upload.
  • Image resolution warnings — replace with a higher resolution picture.

When it says no issues, click Approve, then Save and Continue.

5
Page 3: Rights, Territories & Pricing

Territories: “All territories”. Primary marketplace: Amazon.com. Set the list price — KDP shows the minimum for your page count and what you’ll earn (royalty 60% of list price minus printing cost for paperback). Look at Book 1’s price for guidance. For Kindle: choose the 70% royalty option if the price is $2.99–$9.99.

Optional: order a proof copy (printed at cost, marked “not for resale”) before publishing — strongly recommended. Click “Order proof copy”, check it when it arrives, then come back and publish.

✦A proof copy is the only way to see the real colors and whether the spine text lines up. It usually takes 5–10 days to arrive.
6
Publish

Click Publish Your Paperback Book. The status becomes “In review” and usually goes live in 24–72 hours (can occasionally take longer). You’ll get an email either way. Then repeat the same flow for the Kindle eBook (+ Create → Kindle eBook, or the “Create eBook” link from the paperback’s row), where you can skip ISBN and trim size.

ⓘAfter both are live, Amazon usually links them on one page automatically within a few days. If not, use Contact Us → Combine/link formats in KDP.
5
Categories & keywords — and fixing Book 1
The part that can feel the most confusing. Here’s how to set Book 2 up properly and get Book 1’s categories to change.
1
How categories and keywords work

Categories are the shelves your book appears on (up to 3 in KDP’s picker, for each format). Keywords are the 7 search phrases people type to find a book like yours. Both are set per format — the paperback and the Kindle each have their own categories and keywords, so change both or they’ll disagree.

  • Search Amazon for books similar to yours and see which categories they’re in (scroll down to “Product details → Best Sellers Rank” on their page).
  • Choose a specific category over a broad one — it’s much easier to rank in “Christian Living > Prayer” than in “Religion”.
  • Keywords are short phrases of what readers would search, not single words. Don’t repeat your title or author name — Amazon already uses them.
2
Why Book 1’s categories “won’t take” — fix it

This is almost always one of the reasons below. Work through them in order, and take a screenshot of the page before and after so you can see what changed.

1. The change was never actually saved

  1. Bookshelf → find Book 1 → … → Edit paperback details.
  2. Click Choose categories, pick the new ones, click Save in the pop-up.
  3. That pop-up Save is only temporary. You must scroll to the bottom of the page and click Save and Continue (or “Save as Draft”), then go through to the Pricing page.
  4. On the last page click Publish Your Paperback Book again. The title goes “Live – Changes pending” / “In review” while Amazon applies it.

2. Amazon is still reviewing, or an earlier change is pending

If the status says In review or Changes pending, edits may be locked or silently discarded. Wait until the status is Live, then try once. Category changes can take up to 72 hours to appear on the Amazon page, so check again in 3 days before assuming it failed.

3. She changed one format but not the other

The Kindle eBook and paperback have separate category lists. Edit each: Bookshelf → the Kindle row → Edit eBook details, and repeat the steps above. Then look at which format the Amazon page is showing.

4. The browser is getting in the way

  1. Try a private/incognito window, or a different browser (Chrome, Safari or Edge).
  2. Turn off ad-blockers and extensions for kdp.amazon.com.
  3. Sign out of KDP and back in, then try again.

5. The category she wants isn’t in the list

KDP’s picker only offers a limited set. For others, go to KDP → Help → Contact Us → “Book details” → “Add or change categories” and send Amazon a message: include the title, the ASIN (from the book’s Amazon page) or ISBN, and the exact category paths you want, e.g. “Books > Religion & Spirituality > …”. Amazon support adds them manually, usually within a few days.

6. Still nothing after 3 days

Contact KDP support (Help → Contact Us → Phone callback or email) and send the screenshots. Say: “I saved and published my categories but they have not updated on the live listing.” Have the ASIN ready — a support agent can see what’s stored on their end.

!We can’t see her account from here, so we can’t tell which of these it is. Number 1 (not hitting Save and Continue through to Publish) is by far the most common — try that first.

6
Author essentials — set yourself up as an author
Once Book 2 is live, these free tools do much of the marketing for you.
1
Author Central — one page for both books

Go to author.amazon.com and sign in with the same Amazon account. If you already did this for Book 1, just check that Book 2 appears under Books — if not, click Add books and search the title. (It can take a few days to show.)

ⓘYour Author page gives readers a “Follow” button — they get an email whenever you publish a new book.
2
A+ Content — extra images on the book page

Under KDP’s Marketing tab (next to the book on your Bookshelf), A+ Content lets you add pictures and text below the description. It’s optional but boosts sales. Make images in Canva at the size KDP gives for each module (it’s shown in the editor — common ones are around 970 × 600 px for banner images and 300 × 300 px for small ones). Save as JPG or PNG under 5 MB.
